Accidents tagged with more than one discipline count fractionally toward each in the chart (an accident tagged Ice + Mountaineering adds ½ to each); the report viewer lists every report carrying the tag. In the factor-families view, each report is also split across the families of factors it was tagged with (a report with gear and weather factors adds ½ to each). Outcome is the most severe outcome in the report. 46 non-climbing incidents are excluded.
